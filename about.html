About Sumharbor

Sumharbor is a small project that builds plain, fast calculators for everyday math.

What we build

We focus on calculators people need a few times a year: figuring out a GPA or the score needed on a final, seeing how extra payments shorten a car loan, or checking the margin on a product. Each one is free, needs no account, and works on a phone.

How we work

Every calculator page shows the formula behind the result and a worked example, so you can check the math yourself. We test each calculator against hand-worked examples before publishing it.

Limits

Results are estimates. Your school, lender or accountant may use different rules. We do not give financial, tax, legal or academic advice. Please confirm important numbers with the people who set the rules.

Found a mistake?
