How the final grade formula works

Your current grade covers everything except the final, so it counts for the rest of the course. The final exam covers its weight. To land on your target, the two parts have to add up.

Needed on final = (Target − Current × (1 − Weight)) ÷ Weight

Use the weight as a decimal: 25% is 0.25.

Example: your current grade is 82% and the final is worth 25%. You want 85% overall. Your current work counts for 75% of the course: 82 × 0.75 = 61.5. You need 85 − 61.5 = 23.5 more points from the final, which is worth 25%, so 23.5 ÷ 0.25 = 94%.

Frequently asked questions

What if the answer is over 100%?

Then the grade you want is out of reach with the final alone, unless your instructor offers extra credit. Try a lower target to see what is possible.

What should I enter as my current grade?

Your grade on everything before the final, as your class portal shows it. Make sure it does not already include a placeholder zero for the final.

Does this work if the final replaces a lower grade?

Not directly. Policies like dropping your lowest score change the math. Check your syllabus or ask your instructor.

Can I use letter-grade targets?

Enter the percentage your instructor uses for that letter. The table under the result shows what you would need for 90%, 80%, 70% and 60%.
