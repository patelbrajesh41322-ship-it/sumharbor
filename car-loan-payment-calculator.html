Car Loan Payment Calculator

Estimate your monthly car payment from the price, what you put down, the interest rate and the length of the loan.

How the payment is calculated

The amount you borrow is the price minus your down payment and trade-in. The monthly payment spreads that amount, plus interest, evenly over the term.

Payment = L × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

L is the amount financed, r is the monthly rate (APR ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months.

Example: a $30,000 car with $3,000 down finances $27,000. At 7% APR over 60 months, the payment is about $534.63. Over the whole loan you pay about $5,078 in interest, for a total of about $32,078.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include sales tax and fees?

No. Sales tax, registration, title and dealer fees vary by state and dealer. If you plan to finance them, add them to the vehicle price before you calculate.

What happens if I choose a longer term?

The monthly payment goes down, but you pay more interest in total, and you may owe more than the car is worth for longer. Try a few terms to compare.

What is a good APR for a car loan?

Rates depend on your credit, the lender, the loan term and whether the car is new or used. Compare offers from several lenders, including your bank or credit union.

Can I pay the loan off early?

Often yes. See how much time and interest extra payments could save with our car loan payoff calculator.
