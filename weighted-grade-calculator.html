How a weighted grade works

In many classes, homework, quizzes and exams do not count equally. Each category has a weight, and the weights add up to 100%. Multiply each score by its weight, add the results, and divide by the total weight.

Weighted grade = (score₁ × weight₁ + score₂ × weight₂ + …) ÷ total weight

Example: homework 92% (weight 20%), quizzes 85% (weight 20%) and a midterm 78% (weight 25%). The points are 92 × 20 + 85 × 20 + 78 × 25 = 5,490. The weights so far total 65%, so the grade is 5,490 ÷ 65 = 84.46%. The remaining 35% of the course is still to come.

Frequently asked questions

What if my weights do not add up to 100%?

That is fine while the course is in progress. The calculator shows your grade on the work counted so far and how much weight is still remaining. It will warn you if the weights add up to more than 100%.

How do I find my weights?

Check your syllabus. It usually lists each category, such as homework 20%, quizzes 20%, midterm 25% and final 35%.

What if a category has many assignments?

Average the scores for that category first, then enter the average with the category's weight. If every assignment is worth the same, add up the points earned and divide by the points possible.

How do I see what I need on the final?

Use our final grade calculator. It uses your grade so far and the final's weight to find the score you need.
