Grade Percentage Calculator

Enter the points you earned and the total points possible. We'll give you the percentage and the letter grade.

How the percentage is calculated

Percentage = points earned ÷ points possible × 100

Example: 42 points out of 50 is 42 ÷ 50 = 0.84, which is 84%, a B on the scale below.

Letter grade scale used here

  • A+ = 97% and up
  • A = 93% to 96.99%
  • A- = 90% to 92.99%
  • B+ = 87% to 89.99%
  • B = 83% to 86.99%
  • B- = 80% to 82.99%
  • C+ = 77% to 79.99%
  • C = 73% to 76.99%
  • C- = 70% to 72.99%
  • D+ = 67% to 69.99%
  • D = 63% to 66.99%
  • D- = 60% to 62.99%
  • F = below 60%

Schools and teachers set their own cutoffs. Check your syllabus for the scale your class uses.

Frequently asked questions

Can my score be over 100%?

Yes, if there is extra credit. Enter the points you earned even if they are higher than the points possible.

What if my teacher curves the grade?

A curve changes the points or the cutoffs. Use the curved score as your points earned, or ask your teacher for the new scale.

How do I combine several scores?

Use our weighted grade calculator if categories count differently, or add up all points earned and all points possible if everything counts equally.
