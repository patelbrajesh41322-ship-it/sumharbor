Grade Percentage Calculator

Enter the points you earned and the total points possible. We'll give you the percentage and the letter grade.

How the percentage is calculated

Percentage = points earned ÷ points possible × 100

Example: 42 points out of 50 is 42 ÷ 50 = 0.84, which is 84%, a B on the scale below.

How to work out the points you need

To find how many points you need for a target grade, turn the formula around:

Points needed = target percentage ÷ 100 × points possible

Example: to get 90% on a test worth 60 points, you need 0.90 × 60 = 54 points. To get at least a B (83%) on a 40-point quiz, you need 0.83 × 40 = 33.2 points, so 34 points if scores are whole numbers.

Tips for reading your grade

  • Check the rounding rule. Some teachers round 89.5% up to 90%, others do not. The syllabus or your teacher will tell you.
  • Percentage is not always your course grade. If homework, quizzes and exams count differently, use the weighted grade calculator.
  • Planning ahead. If you want to know what you need on the last exam, try the final grade calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Can my score be over 100%?

Yes, if there is extra credit. Enter the points you earned even if they are higher than the points possible.

What if my teacher curves the grade?

A curve changes the points or the cutoffs. Use the curved score as your points earned, or ask your teacher for the new scale.

How do I combine several scores?

Use our weighted grade calculator if categories count differently, or add up all points earned and all points possible if everything counts equally.

What if my school uses a different grading scale?

Some schools use cutoffs such as 90, 80, 70 and 60 with no plus or minus grades. Others use different cutoffs. The percentage is the same either way. Only the letter changes, so check your syllabus or student handbook for the scale.

Do all assignments count the same?

Not always. If one big exam counts more than a quiz, adding up points will not match your real grade. Use the weighted grade calculator in that case.

Is a percentage the same as a GPA?

No. A percentage shows how many points you earned. A GPA turns your letter grades into points (usually out of 4.0) and averages them across courses, weighted by credits.
