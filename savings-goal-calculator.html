Savings Goal Calculator

Saving for a car, a down payment or an emergency fund? Find out how many months it takes with your monthly deposit and savings account interest.

How the timeline is worked out

The calculator moves forward one month at a time. Each month your balance earns interest at the monthly equivalent of your APY, then your deposit is added. It stops the first month the balance reaches your goal.

Monthly rate = (1 + APY)1/12 − 1
New balance = old balance × (1 + monthly rate) + deposit

Example: a $20,000 goal, $2,000 already saved, $400 a month and a 4% APY takes 3 years and 6 months (42 months). You deposit $16,800 and interest adds about $1,470.

Frequently asked questions

What is APY?

APY is the annual percentage yield: what a savings account pays over a full year including compounding. Rates change, so check your bank for the current one.

What if my account pays no interest?

Set the APY to 0. The result is then simply the amount left divided by your monthly deposit, rounded up to whole months.

Does the result count taxes or inflation?

No. Interest on savings is usually taxable, and prices may rise while you save, so your goal may need to be a bit higher than today's price.
