Tip Calculator

Enter the bill and the tip you want to leave. Split it between friends and see what each person pays.

How the tip is calculated

Tip = bill × tip % ÷ 100
Each person pays = (bill + tip) ÷ number of people

Example: an $80 bill with a 20% tip is $16 of tip and $96 in total. Split four ways, each person pays $24.

Many people in the U.S. tip 15% to 20% at sit-down restaurants. Check whether your bill already includes a service charge or an automatic gratuity, which is common for large groups.

Frequently asked questions

Should I tip on the bill before or after tax?

Both are common. Tipping on the pre-tax amount gives a slightly lower tip. Enter whichever amount you prefer as the bill.

How do I split a bill unevenly?

Calculate each person's share of the bill first, then enter that share with a group size of 1 to get what they owe including tip.

Do tipping customs differ in other countries?

Yes, often a lot. In some countries a service charge is already included and tips are small or not expected. Check local customs when you travel.
