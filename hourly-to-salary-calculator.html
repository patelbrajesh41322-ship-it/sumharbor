How the conversion works

Annual pay is your hourly rate times the hours you work each week times the weeks you work each year. Going the other way, divide an annual salary by the total hours worked in a year.

Annual pay = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year
Hourly rate = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year)

Example: $25 an hour at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is $1,000 a week and $52,000 a year, or about $4,333 a month. An annual salary of $60,000 works out to about $28.85 an hour on the same schedule (2,080 hours).

Frequently asked questions

Is this before or after taxes?

Before. These are gross amounts. Your take-home pay will be lower after taxes, retirement contributions and other deductions.

What if I take unpaid time off?

Lower the weeks worked per year. For example, use 50 weeks if you have two unpaid weeks off.

Why is "every 2 weeks" different from "twice a month"?

Paying every 2 weeks means 26 paychecks a year. Twice a month means 24. The calculator shows both so you can match how you are paid.

Does this include overtime or bonuses?

No. It assumes the same hours every week at one rate.
