Turn an hourly wage into weekly, monthly and yearly pay. Or switch to start from an annual salary and see the hourly rate.

How the conversion works

Annual pay is your hourly rate times the hours you work each week times the weeks you work each year. Going the other way, divide an annual salary by the total hours worked in a year.

Annual pay = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year
Hourly rate = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year)

Example: $25 an hour at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is $1,000 a week and $52,000 a year, or about $4,333 a month. An annual salary of $60,000 works out to about $28.85 an hour on the same schedule (2,080 hours).

Pay periods: weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month

  • Weekly: 52 paychecks a year.
  • Every 2 weeks (biweekly): 26 paychecks a year. Two months a year you receive three paychecks.
  • Twice a month (semimonthly): 24 paychecks a year, usually on fixed dates such as the 15th and the last day.
  • Monthly: 12 paychecks a year.

The yearly total is the same in every case. Only the size of each paycheck changes.

Hourly pay or salary: what to compare

When you compare an hourly job with a salaried one, the hourly equivalent of a salary only tells part of the story. Also look at:

  • Hours you really work. A salaried job with 50-hour weeks pays less per hour than the 40-hour math suggests.
  • Overtime. Under US federal law, most hourly (non-exempt) employees must be paid at least one and a half times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a week. State rules can differ, and some salaried roles are exempt.
  • Paid time off and holidays. Paid leave is part of your pay. Unpaid weeks lower your yearly income.
  • Benefits. Health insurance, retirement matching and bonuses can be worth thousands a year.

Frequently asked questions

Is this before or after taxes?

Before. These are gross amounts. Your take-home pay will be lower after taxes, retirement contributions and other deductions.

What if I take unpaid time off?

Lower the weeks worked per year. For example, use 50 weeks if you have two unpaid weeks off.

Why is "every 2 weeks" different from "twice a month"?

Paying every 2 weeks means 26 paychecks a year. Twice a month means 24. The calculator shows both so you can match how you are paid.

Does this include overtime or bonuses?

No. It assumes the same hours every week at one rate.

Why do some employers use 2,087 hours a year?

A 40-hour week for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours. Some employers, including the US federal government, use 2,087 hours, which spreads the extra days in a calendar year over the pay scale. The difference is small. You can change the weeks per year in the calculator to match your situation.

How do I estimate my take-home pay?

Take-home pay depends on your state, filing status, retirement and insurance deductions. Use your pay stub or your employer's payroll estimate, or a tax tool from your state or the IRS, for a better number.

How do I include overtime?

Work out your regular pay first, then add overtime hours times 1.5 times your hourly rate, if your job pays time and a half. Do this for a typical week and multiply by the weeks you work.

What is the minimum wage?

Minimum wages differ by state and sometimes by city. Check your state labor department for the rate that applies where you work.
