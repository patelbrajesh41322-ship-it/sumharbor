How to calculate a discount

Sale price = original price × (1 − discount ÷ 100)

Example: an $80 item at 25% off costs 80 × 0.75 = $60. You save $20. With 8% sales tax on the sale price, the tax is $4.80 and the total is $64.80.

Sales tax rates differ by state, county and city, and some items are exempt. Enter the rate that applies where you shop, or leave it at 0 to see the price before tax.

Frequently asked questions

Is 20% off then 10% off the same as 30% off?

No. The second discount applies to the already reduced price. 20% off then 10% off is 0.80 × 0.90 = 0.72, which is 28% off in total.

How do I find the original price from the sale price?

Divide the sale price by (1 − discount ÷ 100). A $60 sale price after 25% off means the original was 60 ÷ 0.75 = $80.

Is sales tax charged before or after the discount?

In most cases tax is charged on the discounted price, but store coupons and manufacturer coupons can be treated differently. The register total is the final word.
